Distribution view: boxes

The histogram view becomes Distribution, and Show picks Bars, Boxes or Both. This study compares three ways to draw a box in blip's look, with the tokens of theme.rs at the app's size. Hover a box to see its tooltip.

Show: Boxes

The cycle times of the target's tasks over 20 s, a nominal 1000 µs each. ctrl_loop is tight but now and then late, can_rx jitters evenly, telemetry has long tails. A row per trace along one shared value axis; bins and counts play no part.

Show: Both

Bars as today, and in the strip above them a thin box per trace in place of the mean and σ markers. The box says where the middle half lies; the bars show what the box hides: x and y of robot/pose pile up at their turning points, which no box can tell.

Sideways, next to a plot

Boxes turned on their side beside a plot of motor.speed_rpm. The values run up the shared y range; the names take the place of the time axis, so both plot areas start and end at the same height. One trace makes one box, kept at the largest thickness instead of a slab.

The box tooltip

Laid out like the bin tooltip: names in text_weak on the left, values right aligned in one column as wide as the widest. The trace heads it with its dot. The five numbers of the box are in text, the ends in weak; counts sit under a rule. Values in the trace's number format with the unit.

  • The order follows the picture: low to high upright, high to low sideways. Percentile whiskers are named by their percent ("5 %", "95 %") instead of "Low whisker".
  • Hovering anywhere in a row lights it in hover, as the column of a bin.

Empty, single and crowded

Shown in A. The other options treat these the same way.

Properties

Show comes first, because it decides what else applies. Markers and Whiskers share one slot, so switching Show swaps one row in place. Range moves out of Bins into Values, because it cuts boxes too. Rows that do not apply are hidden, as Axis side is today. New or moved rows are marked blue here.

  • Whiskers is a combo box (1.5×IQR, Min–max, 5–95 %, 1–99 %), as the plot link is: four segments do not fit the 300 pt panel beside the label. Each entry gets a hover text, e.g. "Up to the last sample within 1.5 times the box length".
  • The section that held Orientation and Markers was called Bars; it becomes Display, as it now holds Show.
  • Hidden, not greyed: a greyed Bins section in Boxes would look like it does something. Settings of hidden rows are kept and come back with Bars.

Recommendation

A, washed boxes with names on the axis. A box drawn as a wash under a 1.5 pt outline is the bars' look folded up, so Bars, Boxes and Both read as one view, and the strip of Both is the same box at 6 pt. Names on the category axis make a box plot readable without matching colours to a legend, which matters with six traces of near-equal colour; the legend in the title row stays as in every view.

Caps on the whiskers and translucent dots for outliers keep the classic box plot that firmware developers know. Clamping thickness to 24 pt keeps one or two traces from becoming slabs.

From C, take the 1 px border line between rows if six rows look loose. B's bands draw in the eye too much for a calm tool, and its names exist only as colours.
